### 2.3 Naming: 9 / 10

Local variables are clear: `rawDamage`, `finalDamage`, `variance` (Game.cpp:198-203), `punishDamage` (Game.cpp:126). Minor redundancy: `combat()` takes both a `Character& enemy` and a `std::string& enemyName` (Game.cpp:67), even though `enemy.getName()` would suffice — this suggests the design predates the getter rather than using it.
