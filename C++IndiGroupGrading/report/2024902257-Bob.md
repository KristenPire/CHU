## Overall

"Shadow of the Dark Tower" is a classic dungeon-crawl RPG: Sir Kaelen descends a three-floor cursed tower fighting a Goblin Savage, an Orc Brute, and finally Sir Gideon the Fallen. The structure is clean and complete — enemies, items (potions, a weapon, a trophy armor), win/lose paths, and a flee mechanic all work. The codebase is small, well-named, and compiles without warnings. Points are lost mainly in Clean Code: `combat()` is a 72-line monolith mixing input, attack, flee, and item dispatch; magic numbers are scattered throughout without named constants; and a `displayTitle()` method is declared in `Game.hh` but never implemented or called. No inheritance is used, which costs the full 10-point section.

## Bonus

**Bonus score: 4 / 10**

The game is mechanically sound and plays correctly end-to-end: three floors, escalating enemy stats, two starting potions, a weapon drop that permanently raises ATK, and a trophy item that exists purely as flavour. The flee mechanic with a 40% success chance and a damage penalty for failure is a thoughtful touch. The README is one of the cleaner ones submitted — enemies, items, stats, and win/lose conditions are all listed with specific numbers, which is exactly what was asked.

What holds the bonus back: the game is a minimal but competent execution of the rubric. There is no story beyond the intro paragraph, no room variety, no puzzle or dialogue, and no mechanic that distinguishes the boss from the earlier enemies other than higher numbers. The "Knight's Shield" trophy item is a nice detail but it has no gameplay effect. Ambition is modest — everything works, nothing surprises.
