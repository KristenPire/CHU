## Overall

"Xiao Yan's Adventure" is a Chinese cultivation RPG with a coherent identity — qi pills, HP-cost burst skills, a defend mechanic, and three escalating enemies across a mountain journey. The game's main strength is its creative six-skill system, where spending health to deal burst damage gives combat real stakes. The main weaknesses are a major inventory bug (using one pill empties the whole bag), Windows-only platform code, and no inheritance.
