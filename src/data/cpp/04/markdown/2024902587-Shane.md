## Overall

A God of War RPG where Kratos fights Sentry, Poseidon, Hades, and Zeus in escalating order, with an MP/SP system adding real resource decisions — Chaos Spin hits for 2.5x damage but costs 20 MP, and SP works as a passive dodge stat.
