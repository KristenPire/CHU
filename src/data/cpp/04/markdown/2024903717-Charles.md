  ```cpp

  ```

- **operator<< (Item) absent** (−2) — not declared in Item.hh, not implemented

  Without `operator<<` on Item, Inventory's own operator<< cannot delegate to Item for formatting — it must either format items manually or leave them opaque, defeating the purpose of having items as first-class objects with a stream interface.
