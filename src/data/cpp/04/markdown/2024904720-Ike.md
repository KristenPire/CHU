## Overall

"Dungeon RPG" is an ambitious four-stage dungeon crawler with a four-tier equipment rarity system, dodge mechanic, per-stage stat caps, a heavy strike multiplier, and a replay option — a lot of systems working together. The architectural thinking is strong, with `UI` and `EquipmentLibrary` separated into their own classes.
