## Overall

"Escape from Female Dormitory" is a survival horror RPG built around Chinese dormitory ghost lore, where character stats are framed as a university credit system and the opening choice is whether to open the curtain or pretend to sleep. Three distinct dungeons, a ghost named Xiao Hong, a projector nightmare finale, and an 11-file architecture that genuinely holds together — this is an exceptionally creative project. The main weaknesses are naming conventions and a couple of encapsulation gaps.
