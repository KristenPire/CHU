## Overall

A Pokemon-themed RPG following Pikachu and Charizard trying to rescue Professor Oak from a three-phase boss Pidgey — the personal creative investment shows clearly. Standout features include a Catastropika Z-move mechanic (survive at 1 HP via bond power, then force a KO), a separate Charizard character you control in combat, and a damage preview system that shows estimated damage before you commit to a skill. The main weakness is the boss battle function, which accumulates four boolean state flags and grows to 60 lines.

## Bonus

**Bonus score: 9 / 10**

The Catastropika mechanic — survive at 1 HP via bond power, then force a KO if you pick the right skill — is a faithful recreation of an actual Pokemon Z-move, and it shows real affection for the source material. Professor Oak kidnapped by a Pidgey who becomes a multi-phase boss is exactly the right kind of charming.

Charizard joining as a second character you control separately is a genuine second-character system, not a gimmick. And showing estimated damage before you commit to a skill is a quality-of-life feature most students don't think to include.

**Clean Code note:** The GameBalance namespace with k-prefix named constants is disciplined and makes the game easy to tune — you can see all the balance numbers in one place. The separation of concerns across Game, GameBattle, and GameUi files is clean, and the estimateDamage() vs computeDamage() split (one deterministic for preview, one randomized for real) shows careful thinking about the player experience. The four boolean state flags in runBossBattle() make that function complex, but the rest of the codebase is genuinely well-organized.
