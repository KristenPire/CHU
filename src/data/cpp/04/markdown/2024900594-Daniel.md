## Overall

A dungeon crawler with three fully distinct playable classes — Archer, Berserker, and Necromancer — each feeling mechanically different, especially the Necromancer's ability to raise a fallen enemy as a minion and swap stats with it. The 22-file manager architecture (CombatManager, ShopManager, LevelUpManager, etc.) and exclusive `unique_ptr` usage are technically impressive. The main design smells are repeated `dynamic_cast` calls for Necromancer-specific logic and five `friend class` declarations that break encapsulation.
