### 2.1 Function Size & Focus: 8 / 12

Helper extractions like `distributeRoomLoot()`, `processPlayerAttack()`, and `handleFlee()` would bring both functions under 30 lines and isolate each responsibility clearly.

### 2.2 Canonical Form: 9 / 10

`Inventory` copy constructor (Inventory.cpp:5) and copy-assignment (Inventory.cpp:6–10) delegate correctly to `std::vector`'s own copy semantics, which performs a deep copy of `Item` objects — correct behavior. Self-assignment guard present at Inventory.cpp:7. Destructor is `= default` (Inventory.cpp:12) — correct since `vector` handles its own cleanup. The `Room` class owns two `unique_ptr<Character>` members; because `unique_ptr` is move-only, `Room` is implicitly non-copyable, which is correct. No other class owns resources requiring manual canonical form. Minor deduction: the explicit copy constructor `Inventory(const Inventory& other) : items_(other.items_) {}` is functionally identical to what the compiler would generate — it's harmless but unnecessary, suggesting incomplete understanding of the rule of three vs rule of zero.

No single-letter variables outside loop counters.
